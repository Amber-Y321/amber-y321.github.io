## I use analytics to make complex business decisions easier to act on.

I'm Yahan Yang, an analytics professional with four years of experience across ecommerce, retail, healthcare, and consulting environments. My work sits between business questions and technical execution: I clarify the decision, structure the analysis, build the supporting data product, and communicate what stakeholders should do next.

My experience includes customer and behavioral analytics, revenue and product analysis, marketing measurement, operational simulation, dashboard development, and automated data workflows. I work primarily with SQL, Python, Streamlit, Tableau, APIs, and cloud data platforms.

What I enjoy most is moving beyond a descriptive chart to answer the next question: **Why did this happen, what tradeoff does it create, and which action is most defensible?**

I'm currently interested in Business Analyst, Ecommerce Analyst, Operations Analyst, and Analytics roles where I can combine structured problem solving with hands-on analysis.
