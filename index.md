      <article class="decision-panel panel-executive">
        <p class="decision-question">Is the business healthy—and what needs attention first?</p>
        <p>Connects revenue, orders, customers, returns, and category performance in one executive diagnostic.</p>
        <strong>Decision: prioritize the issue or opportunity to investigate.</strong>
      </article>
      <article class="decision-panel panel-revenue">
        <p class="decision-question">Which customers and products create sustainable revenue?</p>
        <p>Separates volume, value, product mix, customer behavior, and concentration risk.</p>
        <strong>Decision: protect, expand, test, or rationalize the assortment.</strong>
      </article>
      <article class="decision-panel panel-marketing">
        <p class="decision-question">Where should the next marketing dollar go?</p>
        <p>Connects sessions and funnel performance with customer segments and commercial outcomes.</p>
        <strong>Decision: reallocate attention toward valuable audiences and funnel gaps.</strong>
      </article>
      <article class="decision-panel panel-relocation">
        <p class="decision-question">Under what conditions would relocation improve the business?</p>
        <p>Makes scenario assumptions, commercial upside, operating tradeoffs, and uncertainty explicit.</p>
        <strong>Decision: compare location options without hiding risk.</strong>
      </article>
